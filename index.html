01—PROFILE

Computer Vision · Machine Learning

Pengfei Lyu (Lv)

吕鹏飞

Research Fellow at the College of Computer Science, Inner Mongolia University, affiliated with the Spatio-Temporal Intelligence Research Center led by Prof. Zhenwei Shi.

I received my Ph.D. from the Faculty of Robot Science and Engineering, Northeastern University, China, under the supervision of Prof. Chengdong Wu, and worked with Dr. Xiaosheng Yu.

I was also a Joint PhD student at the College of Computing and Data Science, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, supervised by Prof. Jagath C. Rajapakse (IEEE Fellow), working closely with Dr. Pak-Hei Yeung and Dr. Jing Xia.

My research interests include computer vision and machine learning, with a current focus on multimodal collaborative perception in real-world scenarios.
